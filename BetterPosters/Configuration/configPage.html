Poster Style

Studio posters use each title's clean artwork (no text printed on it) with its logo placed over a soft fade at the bottom, and an info line underneath. Titles with no clean artwork on TMDb get the Better Posters one instead.
A tag hanging from the top edge, coloured from the poster itself. The quality badges sit under it.
The title's logo at the bottom. Off (or no logo on TMDb): the title in text.
How dark the fade under the title is. 0 = none, 70 = normal, 100 = black at the very bottom.
The year in the info line under the title (Ratings, Genre and Age Rating below also go in that line).
Empty = the key Jellyfin's own TMDb plugin uses.

Poster Options

Trending, New, IMDb #3 (Better Posters style only)
4K / 1080p, HDR / HDR10+ / Dolby Vision and Dolby Atmos, read from your actual files (the same as Jellyfin's Media Info) and drawn on the poster.
Genre at the bottom
Star rating at the bottom (Studio style: your server's community rating)
PG-13, TV-MA, R

Updating

Off: only libraries where "Better Posters" is ticked under Image fetchers in the library's settings.
New movies and shows (and files you upgrade, e.g. to 4K) get their poster within a minute, not just at the nightly run.
"Update posters now" only touches titles that changed (it also runs every night). "Replace every poster now" makes all of them again. Changing the options above is picked up by the next run on its own.
